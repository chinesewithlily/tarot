Privacy Policy

Chinese with Lily · Lily YouTube Uploader · Last updated 2026-10-28

What this app is

Lily YouTube Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @learn_chinese_with_lily to upload the channel's own videos. It uses YouTube API Services.

Data we access

  • The app requests only the youtube.upload permission, for the owner's own account.
  • It uploads videos and their titles, descriptions and tags to that channel. It does not read, collect, store or share data about any other user.
  • The authorization token is stored privately by the owner and is never shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube terms

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections.
